# Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Decipher ("the App"), operated by Tuckes Dev ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the App.

---

## Information We Collect

- Guesses and game statistics (e.g., attempts, completion status, timestamps) submitted during gameplay
- No names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, or other personal identifiers are collected by the App

## Why We Collect It

- To operate core gameplay features (daily puzzle delivery, results, and aggregate stats)
- To understand aggregate gameplay patterns and improve the App

## How We Process and Share Data

- Gameplay data is stored in Supabase and processed through Supabase Edge Functions
- The app uses local on-device filtering to block inappropriate guess content
- We do not sell data
- We do not share data with third parties for advertising or tracking
- We do not use data for cross-app tracking

## Retention

- Gameplay data is retained only as long as needed for daily game functionality and aggregate statistics

## Children's Privacy

- The App is intended for users 13+; if you are under 13, do not use the App

## Security

- We use reasonable safeguards to protect data; however, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure

## Your Choices

- You may contact us to request deletion of gameplay data associated with your device's recent activity; note that we cannot verify identity beyond device/context information because we do not collect personal identifiers

## Changes to This Policy

- We may update this policy from time to time; material changes will be reflected by updating the date above
